## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Single-module NeoForge 1.21.1 project scaffolding (`ssm.conventions` / `ssm.repositories` convention plugins,
  `gradle.properties`, CI workflow).
- Empty `DeferredRegister` holders and the `ssm` creative tab.
- `SimpleEnergyStorage` (shared FE buffer) and `Capabilities.EnergyStorage.BLOCK` registration scaffold.
- `ssm-common.toml` server config (`SsmConfig`) with per-machine and per-generator capacity/rate/output values.
- Generic processing-machine framework: `MachineConfig`, `MachineRecipeLookup`, `MachineRecipeResult`,
  `SsmMachineRecipe`, `ProcessingMachineState` (unit-testable state machine), `ProcessingMachineBlockEntity`,
  `ProcessingMachineBlock`, and a shared `ProcessingMachineMenu` / `ProcessingMachineScreen`.
- **Crusher**: `ssm:crushing` recipe type, crushed iron/gold/copper items and recipes, block wiring.
- **Sawmill**: `ssm:sawing` recipe type, sawdust byproduct item, block wiring.
- **Smelter**: reuses vanilla `minecraft:smelting` recipes via `SmelterAdapter`, with a flat configurable process
  time instead of vanilla `cookingTime`.
- **Combustion Generator**: ported from `steves-advanced-storage`'s `CoalGeneratorBlockEntity`, with an intentional
  behavioral change (`CombustionBurnState`) that pauses burning instead of wasting fuel when the energy buffer is
  full.
- **Solar Panel**: generates FE during daytime with direct sky access (adapted from `steves-advanced-storage`'s
  Solar Generator).
- **Water Wheel**: generates FE while adjacent to a water source or flowing water (`AdjacentWaterCheck`).
- **Wind Turbine**: generates FE while the column above is clear to the sky.
- Shared `GeneratorBlock<T>` base (block-entity factory, ticker, right-click menu opening) for all 4 generators.
- Push-to-neighbor energy distribution: every generator pushes its buffered FE to all 6 neighbors each tick via
  `Capabilities.EnergyStorage.BLOCK`.
- JEI integration (`SsmJeiPlugin`): custom `ssm:crushing` / `ssm:sawing` recipe categories with catalysts for the
  Crusher and Sawmill; the Smelter is registered as a catalyst for vanilla's own Smelting category instead of a
  redundant `ssm` category.
- `gametest` Gradle subproject with 14 NeoForge gametests covering the Crusher, Sawmill, Smelter, and all 4
  generators end-to-end.
- 22 JUnit unit tests covering `SimpleEnergyStorage`, `ProcessingMachineState`, `SmelterAdapter`'s lookup,
  `CrushingRecipe`, `SawingRecipe`, `CombustionBurnState`, `AdjacentWaterCheck`, and `SkyColumnCheck`.
- `AGENTS.md`, `docs/dev/` subsystem documentation, `README.md`, and this changelog.
